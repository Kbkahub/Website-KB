Myntra Wishlist Conversion

Millions save items but never buy. I found out why, and shipped a solution sized at ₹60 Cr/year.

User ResearchKPI TreeAI/RAG EngineMVPNextLeap Challenge

Problem

Myntra's wishlist has massive saved-item volume but low purchase conversion. The brief: increase 30-day wishlist-to-purchase rate, with no monetary incentives allowed.

Approach

  • Built a TF-IDF + Groq RAG engine to analyze 140 reviews from 7 platforms
  • Affinity-mapped 179 inductive codes into 7 behavioral themes
  • Decomposed conversion into a 4-rate KPI tree
  • Ran 7 depth interviews across 6 user segments

Findings

  • 75% don't organize wishlists. It's a flat dump with zero decision support
  • 62.5% have notifications OFF. Myntra can't reach them
  • 87.5% check competitors. Revenue leaks at every visit
  • Only 2/23 users said a notification triggered purchase

Solution

  • Smart Carousel: in-app wishlisted items with context badges (price drop, low stock). Zero push dependency.
  • Insights Dashboard: price drops, restock alerts, trending items at point of need. Turns the wishlist into a shopping assistant.

Google Photos Retrieval

Users remember photos as moments. Search expects keywords. I redesigned retrieval to close that gap.

Search UXFunnel AnalysisLLM RerankingUsability TestingNextLeap Challenge

Problem

Users who remember a photo but can't describe it precisely fail to retrieve it. 67% of vague-memory users can't even form an effective query. The gap is episodic memory vs keyword search.

Approach

  • Analyzed 183 reviews from 7 platforms using TF-IDF + Groq LLM synthesis
  • Built a 5-stage retrieval funnel (S1 to S5) as a measurable framework
  • Identified 3 user segments by search behavior
  • Tested V1 (keyword) vs V2 (memory-first) with 4 users across 40 tasks

Findings

  • 71% can picture the photo but can't describe it to search
  • 2/7 skip search entirely. They scroll or check WhatsApp instead
  • Problem is surfacing, not recognizing. 5/7 instantly know the right photo
  • Failure concentrates in S1 to S3: attempt, query, click-through

Solution

  • Glow Nudge: search icon glows on aimless scrolling. Non-intrusive entry to search.
  • Trust-building prompts: "Try: that café from our Goa trip" replaces the blank search bar.
  • Follow-up refinement: contextual questions when results don't resolve.
